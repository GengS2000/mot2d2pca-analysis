**Table X. State-dependent community turnover preceding clinical resolution in HMP2**

**A. Participant-level turnover**

| Trajectory | Resolved (n=13) | Persistent (n=5) | δ | *P* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Completed | 0.176 | 0.135 | 0.69 | .026 |
| Raw | 0.362 | 0.290 | 0.17 | .633 |

**B. Adjusted odds of next-bin resolution**

| Trajectory | Immediate aOR (95% CI) | *P* | Earlier aOR (95% CI) | *P* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Completed | 2.75 (1.31–5.78) | .008 | 0.73 (0.28–1.95) | .537 |
| Raw | 1.15 (0.65–2.04) | .634 | 0.77 (0.32–1.86) | .568 |

*Notes.* Panel A reports median participant-level mean Jensen–Shannon turnover; resolved and persistent denote participants with at least one versus no resolving interval, respectively; δ, Cliff's delta; *P*, Mann–Whitney test. Panel B reports adjusted odds ratios (aORs) per SD from logistic models controlling for current activity score, diagnosis, antibiotic exposure, and immunosuppressant exposure, with participant-clustered robust standard errors (immediate: 42 intervals/18 participants; earlier: 33/15). Immediate denotes the four-week interval adjacent to the outcome; earlier denotes one additional interval before it. Resolution was HBI ≤4 for CD or SCCAI ≤2 for UC after a clinically active bin. Results are exploratory and reconstruction-conditional.
