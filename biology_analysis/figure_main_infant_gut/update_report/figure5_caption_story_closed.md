# Figure 5 caption

**Fig. 5. Joint analysis of observed and MOT-(2D)^2 PCA-completed infant-gut tensors recovers cohort-coherent biology, rejects technical artifacts, and distinguishes stable structure from time-localized dynamics.**

**(a)** Sparse subject x time x taxon tensors from DIABIMMUNE and ECAM were analyzed on the observed grid and after MOT-(2D)^2 PCA completion. Each representation was evaluated with LinDA and MaAsLin2, yielding a crossed 2 x 2 reconstruction-by-inference design.

**(b)** Changes in significant-taxon counts from the observed to the MOT-(2D)^2 PCA-completed grid for solid-food timing, delivery mode, feeding mode, and antibiotic exposure. Open and filled points denote observed and completed analyses; arrows connect matched contrasts. Completion changes sensitivity in an inference-dependent manner rather than uniformly increasing discoveries.

**(c)** Literature- and control-anchored scorecard across both inference engines and data representations. Rows are grouped by evidential role: recovery of an expected delivery-mode signal, observed-grid concordance with feeding benchmarks, preservation of a bounded null for antibiotic main effects, and suppression of non-bacterial organellar associations. For *Bacteroides* and *Bifidobacterium*, each cell reports the strongest OTU within the corresponding genus-engine-representation combination; the displayed q-value does not characterize every OTU in that genus. Cell shade indicates -log10(q); triangles show effect direction at q<0.10.

**(d)** Reconstruction changes effect magnitude and calibrated significance differently. For DIABIMMUNE solid-food contrasts, completion increased median absolute effect size by approximately 3.4-fold under both engines, while significant-taxon counts increased from 16 to 45 under LinDA and decreased from 34 to 13 under MaAsLin2 at q<0.10. Similar effect amplification across engines implicates the reconstructed data; divergent discovery counts reflect engine-specific estimation and variance calibration.

**(e)** Biological interpretation from observed-plus-completed triangulation. In ECAM, the observed cesarean-to-vaginal *Bacteroides* ratio increased from 0.36 in year 1 to 0.80 in year 2, whereas the completed trajectory represented the contrast as a nearly constant offset (0.49 to 0.51). Completion therefore recovers the pooled delivery signal but does not establish 24-month persistence. Across exposures, the joint analysis supports a coherent but predominantly first-year delivery imprint, a bounded null for time-averaged antibiotic effects, a feeding-associated maturation-tempo signal rather than a persistent axis, and no reproducible taxon-level axis for individual solid-food timing.

Significance thresholds were q<0.10 for DIABIMMUNE and q<0.05 for ECAM. Full contrast-level results and diagnostic analyses are reported in the Supplementary Information.
